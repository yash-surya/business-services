About Me & The Business

Your One-Stop Partner for Hardware, CAD & Prototyping

Most projects get stalled because hardware development is fragmented—one person does schematic design, another does 3D printing, someone else writes code, and no one assembles the final product.

I solve that by offering an integrated workflow: I design the PCB, hand-assemble and test the circuits, model the mechanical enclosure or jig in CAD, print or machine it, write the firmware, and deliver a complete, fully assembled physical product ready to run.

Engineering Capabilities

Services Offered

Select individual specialized services or combine them for a comprehensive turnkey product build.

PCB Design (2 & 4 Layers)

Schematic capture, compact component placement, impedance control, ground plane optimization, and routing for 2-layer and 4-layer boards with full Gerber and fabrication packages.

Electrical & PCBA Assembly

Manual and semi-automated SMD & through-hole soldering, component mounting, wire harness crimping, continuity validation, and benchtop bring-up debugging.

Turnkey Prototypes (Whole Product)

PCBA + Custom Enclosure = Complete Product. I take your concept from raw idea to a boxed, fully assembled, and functional hardware prototype ready to demo or field test.

Mechanical CAD (Jigs, Fixtures & Robots)

3D modeling for custom enclosures, holding jigs, assembly fixtures, robotic linkages, mechanisms, and 3D print/CNC files in STEP, STL, and DXF formats.

Consultancy & R&D Work

Technical feasibility checks, component selection to prevent supply chain bottlenecks, circuit troubleshooting, schematic reviews, and experimental hardware testing.

College & Hobby Projects

Guiding students, makers, and hobbyists with final year capstone engineering projects, Arduino/ESP32 creations, drone/robot kits, documentation, and prototype builds.

Automation (Home, Process & Industrial)

Smart relay controls, sensor monitoring stations, motor control logic, process sequence automation, and industrial control cabinets with Modbus, RS485, and Wi-Fi/MQTT.

Web Development & IoT Dashboards

Clean and modern responsive websites, device landing pages, and web-based telemetry dashboards to visualize real-time hardware data via WebSockets and REST APIs.
